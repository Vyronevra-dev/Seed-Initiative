Grants & Contracts

Funding and partnership opportunities for organizations advancing youth empowerment, sustainable development, and social innovation.

Our Grant Programs

Seed Initiative offers targeted grant funding to support high-impact initiatives in our focus areas. Our grants are designed to catalyze innovation and scale proven approaches to youth empowerment.

Young Changemaker Grant $5K - $25K

For exceptional young leaders (ages 18-30) with innovative solutions to community challenges. Funding supports project implementation over 12 months, with milestone-based disbursement and post-grant impact reporting.

Chapter Development Grant $10K - $50K

Supports the establishment and growth of new SI chapters and affiliate organizations. Includes seed funding, training curriculum access, and mentorship program enrollment.

Research & Advocacy Grant $15K - $75K

Funds rigorous research on youth development, economic empowerment, and policy advocacy. Recipients join our Global Youth Policy Council and present findings at our annual summit.

Green Ventures Grant $2K - $20K

Dedicated funding for youth-led environmental initiatives and climate action projects. In partnership with the Global Environment Facility, this grant supports scalable green solutions.

Digital Innovation Grant $5K - $30K

Accelerates youth-led tech solutions addressing social challenges. Recipients gain access to our accelerator network, AWS credits, and product development mentorship.

Emergency Response Grant $1K - $10K

Rapid-response funding for youth organizations providing urgent humanitarian assistance. We can deploy funding within 72 hours of application approval.

How to Apply

Step 1: Review Opportunity

Download the full opportunity brief from our grants portal. Each brief includes detailed scope of work, evaluation criteria, budget template, and reporting requirements.

Step 2: Submit Proposal

All applications are submitted through our online portal. You will need to create an account, complete the application form, and upload all required documents in PDF format.

Step 3: Review Process

Proposals are evaluated by our expert review committee using a standardized scoring rubric. Shortlisted applicants may be invited for an interview or presentation.

Step 4: Award & Implementation

Winning proposals are notified within 8 weeks of the deadline. Grants are activated upon signing the funding agreement. Contractors begin work upon receiving the notice to proceed.
