Our Core Programs

We run three interconnected programs that work together to create lasting change for young people and the communities they serve. Each program is designed to be accessible, youth-led, and focused on real-world impact.

Knowledge Programs

Free, accessible education in financial literacy, leadership, entrepreneurship, and civic engagement. Our curriculum is co-designed with youth and delivered both online and in person.

Learn More

Youth Microloan Fund

Small, low-interest loans for young entrepreneurs who lack access to traditional banking. Our peer-guarantee model replaces collateral requirements with community support.

Apply Now

Network Building

Connecting young leaders with peers, mentors, and decision-makers through our Global Youth Summit, local chapter meetups, and digital collaboration platforms.
