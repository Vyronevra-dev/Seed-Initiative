Sponsorship Opportunities

Seed Initiative offers flexible partnership opportunities designed to align with your organizational goals while delivering measurable impact. Every dollar you invest helps young people launch ventures that solve real-world problems.

Annual Summit Sponsor $25,000+

Title sponsorship of our Global Youth Summit, reaching 500+ attendees. Includes keynote speaking slot, booth space, and prominent brand placement across all summit materials.

Training Program Sponsor $10,000+

Sponsor a cohort of 50 young changemakers through our 12-week leadership and entrepreneurship program. Includes your logo on all curriculum materials and a mentor spotlight opportunity.

Microloan Fund Sponsor $5,000+

Fund the next 10 youth-led enterprises with $500 loans each. Includes profile features of funded founders and quarterly impact reports showing revenue growth and job creation.

Chapter Support Sponsor $2,500+

Support a local SI chapter's operations and event programming for a full year. Includes recognition at monthly meetings and social media highlights of chapter activities.

Digital Innovation Lab Sponsor $1,000+

Support our peer-to-peer learning platform and mobile app. Includes your logo on the app splash screen and recognition to all 10,000+ active platform users.

Community Workshop Sponsor $500+

Fund a single community workshop reaching 30 young people. Includes photo coverage from the event and a thank-you message from participants.

Why Partner With Seed Initiative?

Measurable Social Impact: Every partnership comes with a detailed impact report showing exactly how your investment translated into lives changed, from microloan repayment rates to jobs created by youth-led enterprises.

Young Demographic Reach: With 60% of our beneficiaries aged 18-25 and 40% under 30, you gain authentic engagement with Gen Z and young Millennials, consumers who prioritize purpose and social responsibility.

Brand Alignment: Our work in sustainability, financial inclusion, and civic engagement aligns naturally with forward-thinking brands in tech, finance, education, and consumer goods sectors.

Radical Transparency: We publish quarterly financial reports, program evaluations, and beneficiary testimonials. You will know exactly where every dollar goes.
